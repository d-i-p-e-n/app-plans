Privacy Policy

Android and iOS
Effective date: July 22, 2026

This privacy policy describes how this app ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is published by an individual developer ("the developer").

Summary

  • The app does not require an account and the developer does not sell your personal information.
  • The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand usage and diagnose crashes, and Google AdMob to show ads. These services may collect certain data as described below.
  • If the app offers a "Remove Ads" or similar purchase, it is processed by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store; the developer does not see or store your payment details.
  • Where required, you are asked for consent before ads or analytics data collection begin, and you can use the app's core features even if you decline personalized advertising or tracking.

Information the developer collects

The developer does not operate a server or maintain user accounts. Any data described in this policy is collected by the third-party services listed below, not directly by the developer. Calculator inputs, selector choices, and saved preferences (if any) are kept in local storage on your device and are not transmitted to the developer unless a specific feature states otherwise.

Analytics and crash reporting (Firebase)

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to diagnose crashes. These services may collect data such as:

  • device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language, and approximate location derived from IP address);
  • usage events (screens viewed, features used, session length);
  • crash logs and diagnostic information.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase and Privacy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If the app displays ads, it uses Google AdMob. In the course of serving ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • app interaction information related to ad serving (for example, ad views and taps);
  • diagnostic and performance information.

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

If the app offers an in-app purchase (for example, to remove ads), purchases are handled entirely by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. The developer receives confirmation that a purchase was made but does not receive or store your payment card details.

Consent and your choices

  • Consent message (GDPR and similar laws). If you are in a region where consent is required, the app shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads or collecting analytics data. You can decline or manage your choices, and you can revisit them at any time via the "Privacy options" control in the app where it is shown.
  • Non-personalized ads. If you decline personalized advertising, the app requests non-personalized ads instead.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS). On iOS, the app asks for tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you deny it, the app does not access the IDFA and remains fully functional.
  • System-level controls. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Android or iOS privacy settings.

Declining consent or tracking never limits the core features of the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and security

The developer retains no user data directly. Data collected by Google for analytics and advertising is retained and secured according to Google's policies linked above. Data stored locally on your device remains under your control and is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
